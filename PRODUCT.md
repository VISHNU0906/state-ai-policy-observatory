# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Policy researchers and assignment reviewers assessing AI-related Indian state-government activity at a laptop in a well-lit office. They need to scan policy milestones, inspect source provenance and reproduce a dataset.

## Product Purpose

Make a bounded, thoroughly sourced research corpus understandable and auditable. The main task is filtering actions by state, category and implementation stage, then reading the underlying evidence.

## Brand Personality

Measured, precise, open. Calm confidence grounded in evidence.

## Anti-references

Avoid promotional AI imagery, leaderboards, decorative maps implying national coverage, inflated impact statistics, and dashboards that bury source links.

## Design Principles

Evidence is one click away. Announcements and implementation must remain visibly distinct. Unknown dates and coverage gaps are part of the product. Counts describe the sample only. Downloadable records and reproducibility matter as much as appearance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable native controls, semantic headings and tables, mobile reflow, visible focus and no required animation. Status never depends on colour alone.
